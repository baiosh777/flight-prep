Flights

Flights are stored on this iPad only. Everything works without internet once a flight is loaded.

How to useoffline
  1. Claude sends a flight file for every flight (for example EK794_NBJ-DXB_flight.json). Save it to the Files app.
  2. Tap Load flight file and pick it. The app opens the flight on the NOW screen.
  3. At take-off tap AIRBORNE NOW (or type the time). Tap waypoint rows as you pass them; tap Done on calls.
  4. Your take-off time, ticks and fuel entries stay on this iPad, per flight.

Preparation aid only – the OFP, official Emirates documentation and ATC prevail. Times are planned EETs from the T/O time you set; the app does not know the aircraft position.
